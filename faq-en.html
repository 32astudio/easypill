Notifications Related

1. Why are my notifications delayed?

Android prioritizes battery life and performance, which can sometimes delay apps running in the background. Common reasons include:

  • Doze Mode: An Android power-saving feature that limits background activity when your phone is idle. It may group notifications into "maintenance windows," causing delays.
  • Battery Optimization: Many apps are restricted by default to save power, limiting how often they can "wake up" your device.
  • Manufacturer Restrictions: Brands like Samsung, Xiaomi, and Huawei often add extra power management layers that aggressively close background processes.
  • Device Factors: Older devices, low battery, or "Force Closing" the app can also prevent alerts from firing on time.
Note: These are system behaviors outside of EasyPill’s direct control, but they can usually be fixed by adjusting your settings.
↑ Back to top

2. How do I check if my app is affected by battery optimization?

To fix this, you need to set EasyPill to "Unrestricted" or "Not Optimized."

  • Stock Android (e.g., Pixel): Go to Settings > Apps > EasyPill > Battery and select Unrestricted.
  • Samsung: Go to Settings > Apps > EasyPill > Battery and select Unrestricted.
  • Xiaomi: Go to Settings > Battery & Performance > App battery saver > EasyPill and select No restrictions.
↑ Back to top

3. What about manufacturer-specific settings?

Different brands have unique tools that may block notifications. Here are common fixes:

  • Xiaomi/Redmi (MIUI): Enable Autostart in App settings. Lock the app in your recent apps tray.
  • Huawei/Honor: In Battery settings, find App Launch, select EasyPill, and manage it manually (enable Auto-launch, Secondary launch, and Run in background).
  • OnePlus: Go to Battery optimization and select Don't optimize for EasyPill.
  • Oppo/Vivo: Allow background activity in the App Info settings.
Tip: If these steps don't match your phone, search Google for "[Your Phone Model] allow background apps."
↑ Back to top

4. Do I need to grant specific permissions?

Yes. For the best reliability, ensure the following:

  • Notification Permissions: Allow sound, vibration, and lock screen visibility in your phone settings.
  • Alarms & Reminders (Android 12+): Allow this permission under App Info.
  • Do Not Disturb: Ensure your phone isn't in a Focus Mode that silences alerts.
↑ Back to top

5. What if notifications still don't work?

  • Don't Force-Close: Swipe-closing the app from your recent apps list can kill background processes. Keep it running in the background.
  • Update: Ensure both your Android OS and the EasyPill app are up to date.
  • Contact Us: If issues persist, please contact support with your device model and Android version so we can investigate further.
↑ Back to top

6. Are there limitations to scheduled notifications?

Android does not guarantee exact timing down to the second due to power-saving features. While we strive for precision, slight delays can occur. Additionally, restarting your phone may require the app to be opened once to reschedule your alerts.

OCR (Label Scanning) Related

1. Why does the OCR function have limited free usage?

Our OCR (Optical Character Recognition) feature relies on advanced third-party AI infrastructure, which incurs costs for every scan. Therefore, the free version has a usage limit. You can unlock more scans by watching ads or subscribing to our monthly service for unlimited, convenient access.

↑ Back to top

2. Is the OCR function multilingual?

Yes. The OCR scanner can read labels in all languages currently supported by EasyPill (check the Settings menu for the list). However, please note that while it reads the text, it does not translate the label into a different language.

↑ Back to top

3. Can I rely solely on the OCR output for medical information?

No. While our OCR is rigorously tested, factors like lighting, label damage, or font styles can affect accuracy. Furthermore, a label may contain general info that doesn't apply to your specific dosage instructions.

  • Always Verify: Carefully review the scanned text and edit it as needed before saving.
  • Consult a Pro: If you are unsure about a medicine's instructions, always consult your doctor or pharmacist. Do not rely solely on the app for critical medical decisions.
